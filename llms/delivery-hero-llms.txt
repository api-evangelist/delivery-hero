# Delivery Hero — On Demand Rider API

> Delivery Hero SE is a Berlin-based multinational online food-delivery and quick-commerce
> company operating in 70+ markets through brands including foodpanda, Talabat, Glovo,
> PedidosYa, Yemeksepeti and Hungerstation. Its public developer surface exposes logistics
> and partner-integration APIs. The On Demand Rider (ODR) API lets merchants request
> on-demand courier delivery (create outlets, estimate fees/times, create and track orders,
> retrieve proof of pickup/delivery/return) across brands such as Glovo and pandago.

## APIs
- [On Demand Rider API](https://on-demand-rider-docs.deliveryhero.io/): OpenAPI 3.0.0, v1.1.1. Create/track on-demand delivery orders and manage outlets.
- [Q-Commerce Order Transmission & Assortment API](https://developers.deliveryhero.com/documentation/qc-API-integration.html): receive orders into POS and sync grocery assortment/promotions.
- [Restaurant Integration (POS) API](https://developers.deliveryhero.com/documentation/pos.html): order transmission for restaurant partners.

## Auth
- OAuth 2.0 client_credentials with JWT-bearer assertion. Token endpoint: https://sts.deliveryhero.io/oauth2/token
- Scopes follow the pattern `{brand}.api.{country_code}.*` (e.g. `pandago.api.sg.*`).
- Requests carry `Authorization: Bearer {access-token}`.
